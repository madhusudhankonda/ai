3-Hour Agenda

Minimal slides. Maximum doing. Each part builds on the last.

0:00 – 0:30

Part 1 — Understanding AI Products

What is different when a product uses LLMs, RAG, agents or automation. Why outputs are not guaranteed. Why human review, confidence and fallback behaviour matter — and how that changes your artefacts from day one.

0:30 – 1:00

Part 2 — AI Opportunity Discovery

How to spot good, bad and risky AI use cases. A simple framework for moving from "we need AI" to a clear, justified product opportunity — with the right scope and the right questions for your stakeholders.

1:00 – 1:45

Part 3 — Requirements for AI Systems

The core mindset shift. Instead of only writing "the system shall return the correct answer," you now need to think about:

  • Cited sources and confidence indicators
  • Fallback behaviour and human escalation paths
  • Audit trail and review requirements
  • Data quality, privacy and safety guardrails
1:45 – 2:45

Part 4 — Backlog Creation Workshop

Take one business problem. With AI as your assistant, produce the full artefact set: problem statement · stakeholder map · user personas · journey map · epics · user stories · acceptance criteria · NFRs · risks · MVP scope · test scenarios.

2:45 – 3:00

Part 5 — Working with AI Engineering Teams

Enough vocabulary to ask better questions and collaborate confidently: RAG, prompts, agents, model selection, evaluations, hallucination, guardrails, cost, observability, privacy and security. Not deep technical — just enough to be useful.
