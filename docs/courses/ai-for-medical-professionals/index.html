Course Curriculum

10 modules — Modules 1–4 build confidence, 5–8 engage real clinical judgement, and 9 makes it personal to your specialty

1️⃣

What Even Is AI?

Demystifying the buzzword: what AI is and isn't, how it differs from a Google search, and what it's actually good at — pattern recognition, prediction and generation.

2️⃣

Your First AI Tools

ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude — which to use when, prompting basics, and real use cases: referral letters, summarising papers, explaining diagnoses and pressure-testing a differential.

3️⃣

Basics of Generative AI

What Generative AI and LLMs really are, the prominent frontier models, and a hands-on demo of LLMs in action.

4️⃣

AI Already in Your Clinic

Tools you may already use: eSanjeevani, radiology and ECG AI, drug interaction checkers, Practo/DocsApp, and the Ayushman Bharat / ABDM stack.

5️⃣

Documentation & Admin

Tackling the documentation burden: ambient AI scribing, dictation to structured notes, discharge summaries — what's available in India now vs. what's coming.

6️⃣

AI as a Clinical Thinking Partner

A second opinion, not a replacement: clinical decision support, stress-testing a diagnosis, chronic disease and oncology — and avoiding the "AI said so" trap.

7️⃣

Can I Trust This?

Critical evaluation: reading AI claims, what "FDA cleared" / "CE marked" mean, the Indian patient data problem, hallucinations, and a 5-question adoption checklist.

8️⃣

Ethics, Law & Liability

Who's responsible when AI is wrong, patient consent, data privacy under the DPDP Act 2023, bias, and where NMC regulation is heading.

9️⃣

AI in Your Specialty

Pick your track: General Practice, Radiology & Pathology, Cardiology, Oncology, Mental Health or Telemedicine.

🔟

Becoming an AI-Informed Clinician

Stay current without the hype, evaluate new tools, advocate for responsible AI in your hospital, and leave with a 30-day action plan.
